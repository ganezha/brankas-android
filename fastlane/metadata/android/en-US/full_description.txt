Brankas is a private vault for photos, videos, and notes, stored locally on your device.

Keep personal items in an encrypted vault protected by a PIN or passphrase. You can also enable biometric unlocking on your device.

BACK UP AND RECOVER
Create an encrypted backup protected with your recovery code. The backup does not contain your PIN. To restore it, enter the recovery code, confirm the backup, and create a new PIN. Brankas verifies the backup before replacing the current vault and keeps the old vault available until the restore succeeds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account required
• No ads, analytics, or tracking
• No internet permission; vault data stays on your device
• Export backups through the Android file picker to a location you choose

Keep your recovery code somewhere safe and separate from your device. It is required to restore a backup if you lose access to your vault.
